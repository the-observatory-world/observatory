# The Observatory

**Independent AI Systems Research · Experimental Architecture · Open Inquiry**

The Observatory is an independent research initiative dedicated to investigating language-model behavior, semantic coherence, relational continuity, and the influence of environments on complex systems.

Our work brings together longitudinal studies, mathematical frameworks, experimental protocols, and distributed AI architectures, including the **Semantic Coherence Index (SCI)** and **Zero-Point Protocol (ZPP)**.

The Observatory maintains a public Research Archive documenting its studies, technical publications, experiments, and exploratory probes.
